BOXED WARNING
Metformin-associated lactic acidosis is a serious but rare adverse reaction. Discontinue metformin if lactic
acidosis is suspected. Risk may be increased in patients with renal impairment, hypoxic states, or
conditions associated with tissue hypoperfusion.
CONTRAINDICATIONS
Metformin is contraindicated in patients with severe renal impairment and in patients with a known
hypersensitivity to metformin.
