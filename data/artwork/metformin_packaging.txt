Document Name: Metformin Product Label and Packaging Artwork
Document Type: Artwork
Product: Metformin Hydrochloride Tablets

The product packaging and prescribing artwork contains key safety information for Metformin Hydrochloride Tablets.

The artwork includes a boxed warning regarding metformin-associated lactic acidosis. It informs healthcare professionals that lactic acidosis can be serious and may result in severe outcomes.

The prescribing artwork also includes contraindications related to severe renal impairment and hypersensitivity to metformin.

Any significant update to boxed warnings, contraindications, or critical safety information must trigger a review of this artwork to determine whether the approved product information requires revision.
