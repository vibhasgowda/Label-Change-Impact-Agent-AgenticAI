Document Name: Metformin Safety Training Material
Document Type: Training Material
Product: Metformin Hydrochloride Tablets

This training material is used to educate relevant teams about the safe use and regulatory requirements of Metformin Hydrochloride Tablets.

The training covers metformin-associated lactic acidosis, symptoms and risk factors, renal impairment, contraindications, and appropriate patient monitoring.

Personnel should understand the importance of renal function assessment and the management of patients who may be at increased risk of renal impairment.

Changes involving new safety warnings, additional monitoring requirements, contraindications, or risk-management instructions should trigger a review and possible update of this training material.
